How it works

Three steps to $1,000

  1. 1

    Pick one team name

    Choose a name your whole organization will recognize. Keep it simple so nobody spells it differently.

  2. 2

    Everyone registers under it

    When members sign up for Gladiator Dash, they enter that exact team name during registration.

    Team nameYour org name

  3. 3

    Most sign-ups wins

    The organization with the most runners registered under its team name in each category wins $1,000 for its philanthropy.

Why it matters

The kids at Still Creek Ranch face obstacles they didn’t choose. Every day, they overcome them.

Gladiator Dash benefits Still Creek Ranch. The Giveback Program sends $1,000 back to the organizations that bring the most runners, so your philanthropy wins too.
