General

7 questions

Do I have to do every obstacle?

No. Every obstacle is challenge by choice, with a way around each one. Anyone can start, and everyone can finish.

I’m not an athlete. Should I still sign up?

Absolutely. The course is built so runners of every skill level can take part and have a blast.

I can’t swim. Can I still participate?

Yes. There’s one swim station on the course, and like every obstacle it’s challenge by choice. Take the alternate route and skip the water entirely.

What if it rains?

Rain only makes the Dash better, so come ready to get dirty. If the weather turns dangerous, we’ll make a final call one hour before the start.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. You’re responsible for your dog the whole time; One Army and Gladiator Dash aren’t responsible for pets.

When do I get my T-shirt?

Shirt and bib pickup opens one hour before the first heat on race day. We also hold two pickup days the week before, so you can skip the race-day line.

Can friends and family come watch?

Please do. Spectators are encouraged, parking is free, and many obstacles are easy to see from the sidelines. Bring a camera.

Race Day

7 questions

When is Gladiator Dash?

April 11, 2027. Heats start at 8:00 a.m. and go out every 10 minutes into the early afternoon.

Where is it?

Lake Bryan, 8200 Sandy Point Rd, Bryan, TX 77807.

When should I arrive?

At least 45 minutes before your heat, so you have time to park, check in and get ready.

What should I bring?

A photo ID and your wallet. We recommend an extra change of clothes, shoes and a towel. Stick around after your race for live music and food trucks.

Is there somewhere safe to leave my things?

Yes. There’s a secure gear check at the check-in tables, including a key wall. You can also leave valuables in your car.

Where do I park?

Parking at Lake Bryan is free for runners and spectators.

Do I have to sign a waiver?

If you registered online, you already accepted the waiver. If you sign up on race day, you’ll sign one at the registration table acknowledging that the race carries risks, including personal injury. Please read it before you sign.

Registration

4 questions

How do I register a team, or join one?

Pick a team name and make sure everyone on the team enters it exactly the same way during registration. Signing up with a student organization? Your team name also counts toward the Giveback Program.

Is there a minimum or maximum team size?

No. Your team can be as big or as small as you want.

Can I sign up on race day?

Yes. Race-day sign-ups are $30. Register now for a lower early price.

Is there an age requirement?

Runners must be at least 18. Younger gladiators need a parent or guardian to sign a waiver at the registration table, and that adult must either run with them or stay at the event the whole time they’re there.

Competitive & Endurance

3 questions

What are competitive heats?

Competitive heats let you race for time, placement and medals, with official timing and a live leaderboard in the Gladiator Arena. The top three men and top three women in every competitive heat get medals, and the overall winner is crowned after the final competitive heat.

In 2026 there were four: a Corps Competitive Heat at 8:00 a.m. and Open Competitive Heats at 8:10, 8:50 and 9:00 a.m.

Is there a heat for the Corps of Cadets?

Yes. The Corps Competitive Heat runs first, followed by the Open Competitive Heats.

What’s the Endurance Race?

Run the course as many times as you can. The Endurance Race starts in the morning and runs until 1:00 p.m. Each finished lap gets a tally by your name, a water table sits about halfway through the course, and whoever completes the most laps wins a prize.
