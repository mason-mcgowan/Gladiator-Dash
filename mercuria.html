Who is Mercuria

Mercuria is one of the world’s leading independent energy and commodity groups. Founded in 2004 by Marco Dunand and Daniel Jaeggi, it is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, and runs its North American operations from Houston, Texas.

More than 1,300 employees work across about 50 countries, trading crude oil and refined products, natural gas and LNG, power, base metals and agricultural goods.

The company’s mission is to connect markets and help the world move toward more sustainable energy.

What Mercuria does

Mercuria connects energy markets around the world, working toward energy that is more sustainable, affordable and reliable.

  • Trading

    Connecting the places that produce energy with the markets that need it.

  • Financial structuring

    Putting together the financing behind energy deals.

  • Asset investment

    Investing in the energy assets and infrastructure that keep markets supplied.
